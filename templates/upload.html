How resume analysis works

The same 5 real steps shown in the pipeline card, explained.

  1. PDF parsing — your file is downloaded from secure storage and its raw text extracted.
  2. NLP & entity extraction — an LLM reads that text and pulls out skills, education, experience, projects, and certifications — nothing invented, only what's actually in your resume.
  3. AI Job Agent matching — your resume is compared against real job postings already in the pool using embedding similarity, not keyword matching.
  4. Role & salary insight — once matching has real results, your best-fit roles and salary context are derived directly from those matches.
  5. Dashboard ready — everything above lands on your dashboard, kept up to date as the AI Job Agent keeps scanning.
